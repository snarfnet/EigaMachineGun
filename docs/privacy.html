Privacy Policy - Movie Machinegun

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Information Collection

Movie Machinegun does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app does not require user registration or login.

Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services:

  • iTunes Search API - To retrieve movie information. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • Google AdMob - To display advertisements. Google's privacy policy applies. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization.

Data Storage

Favorite movies are stored locally on your device only. No data is transmitted to external servers.
